Accountant Access

Applies to: Business and Enterprise tiers only. The accountant portal is not available on Free or Pro.

Give your accountant (or CPA) read access to your expense data so they can review and prepare filings without needing your login.

How do I invite my accountant?

  1. Go to Settings → Accountants.
  2. Select Invite Accountant.
  3. Enter their email address.
  4. Select Send Invite.

Your accountant receives an email to create their own accountant-role account and connect to yours.

How do I see what my accountant can access?

  1. Go to Settings → Accountants.
  2. Select the connected accountant to view their access level.
  3. Accountants can view expenses, receipts, trips, and reports, but cannot edit them.

How do I remove an accountant's access?

  1. Go to Settings → Accountants.
  2. Select the accountant you want to disconnect.
  3. Select Remove Access.

How do I switch between client accounts (as an accountant)?

  1. Go to the Accountant Dashboard.
  2. Select the client you want to view from your client list.
  3. You're switched into a read-only view of that client's data.
