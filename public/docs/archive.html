Archive

Applies to: All tiers (Free, Pro, Business, Enterprise).

Find expenses, trips, and reports that have been archived or removed from your active lists.

How do I find an archived item?

  1. Go to Archive.
  2. Browse or search the list of archived expenses, trips, and reports.
  3. Select an item to view its details.

How do I restore an archived item?

  1. Go to Archive.
  2. Select the item you want to restore.
  3. Select Restore. It reappears in its original active list (Expenses, Trips, etc.).

How does an item end up in the Archive?

Items move to the Archive when you delete them from their active list, or when they're automatically archived after a report they belong to is finalized. Archived items are not permanently deleted.
