Notifications

Applies to: All tiers (Free, Pro, Business, Enterprise). Approval-related notifications only apply if you're on Business or Enterprise.

Stay on top of approval updates, processed trips, and account alerts.

How do I view my notifications?

  1. Go to Notifications.
  2. Unread notifications are highlighted; select one to view details and jump to the related item (expense, trip, or approval).

How do I mark a notification as read?

  1. Go to Notifications.
  2. Select the notification, or use Mark all as read to clear the whole list.

How do I control which notifications I receive?

  1. Go to Settings → Notifications.
  2. Toggle the notification types you want to receive or turn off.

See Settings for the full list of notification options.
