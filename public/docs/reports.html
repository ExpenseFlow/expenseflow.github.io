Reports

Applies to: Pro, Business, and Enterprise tiers. Report export and tax-ready formats (CRA/IRS) are not available on the Free tier.

Turn your tracked expenses and mileage into audit-ready reports for reimbursement, taxes, or your own records.

How do I generate an expense report?

  1. Go to Reports.
  2. Select the date range and the expenses/trips you want to include.
  3. Select Generate Report.
  4. Review the summary, then export or share it (see below).

How do I export a report?

  1. Open the report from Reports.
  2. Select Export.
  3. Choose your format (e.g. PDF or CSV).
  4. The file downloads to your device (mobile: it's saved to your device's downloads or shared via the share sheet).

How do I view tax-specific reports?

  1. Go to Reports.
  2. Select the Tax tab.
  3. Choose the tax year and jurisdiction-specific format (e.g. CRA or IRS), where available.
  4. Select Generate Report.
Tax report formats are only available for supported jurisdictions. Other jurisdictions can still generate general expense reports.

How do I reconcile a report against my records?

  1. Go to Reports.
  2. Select the Reconciliation tab.
  3. Compare the listed expenses/trips against your bank or card statement.
  4. Flag or edit any mismatches directly from the reconciliation view.
