Invoices & Expense Reports

Invoices & Expense Reports

Applies to: Pro, Business, and Enterprise tiers. Exporting bundled reports is not available on the Free tier.

Bundle related expenses into a single invoice or expense report for billing or reimbursement.

How do I create a new expense report?

  1. Go to Invoices.
  2. Select New Expense Report.
  3. Choose the expenses and trips to include.
  4. Add any report-level details (client, project, notes).
  5. Select Save.

How do I send an expense report to a client or employer?

  1. Open the expense report from Invoices.
  2. Select Export or Share.
  3. Choose the format and destination (download, email, or share sheet on mobile).

How do I edit an expense report before sending it?

  1. Open the expense report.
  2. Add, remove, or edit the included expenses/trips.
  3. Select Save.
Once a report has been exported or sent, edit it and re-export to keep the recipient's copy in sync — ExpenseFlow does not update sent copies automatically.
