Receipts

Receipts

Applies to: All tiers (Free, Pro, Business, Enterprise). Free is limited to 5 receipts/month; Pro and above are unlimited.

Capture receipts and attach them to your expenses so every claim is backed by documentation.

How do I add a receipt?

  1. Go to Receipts.
  2. Select Add.
  3. Take a photo of the receipt, or upload an existing image/PDF.
  4. Confirm the upload.
On mobile: the camera opens directly so you can snap the receipt in the moment — no need to leave the app to use your phone's camera roll first.

How do I attach a receipt to an expense?

  1. Open the expense from Expenses.
  2. Select Attach Receipt.
  3. Choose an existing receipt from your Receipts list, or add a new one.
  4. Select Save.

How do I view a receipt?

  1. Go to Receipts.
  2. Select the receipt you want to view.
  3. The receipt image opens for you to review; use the zoom controls if the text is hard to read.

How do I remove a receipt from an expense?

  1. Open the expense with the attached receipt.
  2. Select the receipt, then select Remove.
  3. Confirm removal. The receipt itself stays in your Receipts list and can be reattached later.
