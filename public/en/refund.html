Refund Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-11

This Refund Policy describes how billing, cancellation, and refunds work for ExpenseFlow subscriptions. It supplements our Terms of Use.

Billing

Subscription fees are billed in advance for each billing period (monthly or annual, depending on your plan) and charged to the payment method on file.

Cancellation

You can cancel your subscription at any time from your account settings. When you cancel, your subscription remains active until the end of the billing period you've already paid for. You will not be charged again after that period ends.

Refunds

We do not provide refunds or credits for partial billing periods, including when you cancel, downgrade, or don't use the service during a period you were billed for.

Questions

If you believe you were charged in error, contact us through the Contact page.
