Common questions

What is the CRA per-km rate for 2026?add

For 2026 the prescribed rate is 73 cents per km for the first 5,000 km driven in the year and 67 cents per km after that in the provinces. In Yukon, the Northwest Territories and Nunavut it is 77 and 71 cents.

Can self-employed people use the per-km rate?add

The CRA per-km rate is the amount an employer can pay an employee tax-free as a reasonable allowance. Self-employed people filing a T2125 generally claim actual motor vehicle expenses × business-use percentage, though many use the per-km rate as a rough benchmark. Check with your accountant.

Is a flat monthly car allowance taxable?add

Generally yes. An allowance that is not based on the kilometres driven is a taxable benefit, and it cannot be averaged out at year end to look like a per-km rate.

What does the CRA want in a logbook?add

For each business trip: the date, destination, purpose and kilometres driven, plus the total kilometres driven in the year. A full 12-month logbook can set your business-use percentage for later years under the simplified method.

Can I deduct my commute?add

Generally no. Travel between home and a regular place of work is personal. Trips from your workplace or home office to clients and job sites can qualify.
