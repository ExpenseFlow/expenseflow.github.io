CRA logbook requirements for vehicle expenses

To claim vehicle expenses against business income, the CRA expects a logbook that shows how much you drove for business. Here is what to record and how to keep it without the busywork.

Home › Guides › CRA logbook requirements

What a CRA-ready logbook contains

To support a motor vehicle expense claim, keep a record that shows, for each business trip:

  • Date of the trip
  • Destination
  • Purpose of the trip
  • Kilometres driven

You also need the odometer reading at the start and end of the fiscal period, so total kilometres for the year can be established.

Calculating your business-use percentage

Business use is your business kilometres divided by your total kilometres for the year. That percentage is applied to eligible vehicle costs such as fuel, insurance, maintenance and capital cost allowance. Without a logbook, the claim is hard to support.

Is a sample logbook allowed?

The CRA allows a representative sample in some cases: after keeping a full logbook for a year, you may be able to use a sample period (such as three months) in following years if your driving pattern stays similar. Check the current CRA guidance before relying on this approach.

Keep your records

The CRA generally expects you to keep records for six years from the end of the tax year they relate to, so store your logbook and receipts somewhere safe and searchable.

Frequently asked questions

Does the CRA require a logbook for vehicle expenses?

If you claim motor vehicle expenses, you need records showing your business kilometres and total kilometres. A logbook with date, destination, purpose and kilometres is the standard way to show this.

Can I use an app instead of a paper logbook?

Yes. The CRA cares about the content and reliability of the record. An app that records trips as they happen and lets you note the purpose can meet the requirement.

How long should I keep my logbook?

The CRA generally expects records to be kept for six years from the end of the tax year they relate to.
