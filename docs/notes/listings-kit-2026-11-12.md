## Per-platform steps (need an account owner: these cannot be automated)
1. **Product Hunt**: create a maker account, "Submit" a product. Use the tagline/description above, add 3-4 screenshots and a
   maker comment. Schedule a launch day, ask a few users to upvote and comment. Link: www home page.
2. **G2**: claim a seller profile at sell.g2.com (needs a work-email). Category: Expense Management. Fill in the profile,
   then invite 5-10 real customers to review. Do not buy or incentivize reviews beyond what G2 policy allows.
3. **Capterra** (Gartner Digital Markets): add the product at vendors.capterra.com (free listing). Same copy and category. Capterra
   also syndicates to Software Advice and GetApp, which gives extra backlinks.
4. **Google Business Profile**: only eligible if there is a staffed physical location or service area. For a software
   company with no storefront it may be refused. If the Aryabhatta Labs office qualifies, create the profile for the company
   and set the website to the www URL; otherwise skip it.
5. **Also worth doing**: Crunchbase, AlternativeTo (list as an alternative to MileIQ/Everlance/TripLog), SaaSHub, Indie Hackers,
   and an Apple/Google developer-site "More apps" link on aryabhatta.ca pointing to the www URL.

## Rules of thumb
- Use the exact same name, tagline and URL everywhere (consistent citations).
- Link to the www host and to deep pages (calculator, guides), not only the home page.
- Reviews and ratings must come from real users; add `aggregateRating` structured data only once real ratings exist.
